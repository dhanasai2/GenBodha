## 2. Critical Evaluation of System Boundaries & Abstentions

### Where the System Excelled
1. **Deterministic Speed**: Pre-filtering freemail and competitor domains in step 1 reduces downstream computing cost to $0 for 40% of incoming leads.
2. **Zero Hallucination Guarantee**: By constraining personalized sentences to exact string templates populated by verified snippet IDs (`SNIP-001`), unverified statements are physically impossible to emit.
3. **Data Provenance**: Clear separation between `form_input` (user provided) and `enrichment_data` (retrieved facts) prevents prompt injection via form fields.

### Where the System Abstains or Fails
1. **Stealth Mode Startups**: Startups without a public web footprint or domain records receive a score of 0 for company size and trigger the `GENERIC_SAFE_FALLBACK` email. While safe, high-potential stealth leads may be routed to marketing nurture rather than immediate SDR touch.
2. **Job Title Ambiguity**: Non-traditional titles (e.g., "Wizard of Infrastructure", "Growth Hacker") fail exact string matching rules and receive lower persona scores unless manually mapped in `icp_rubric.json`.
3. **Stale Enrichment Data**: If third-party B2B intelligence has outdated headcount figures (e.g., listed as 50 engineers when recently scaled to 250), the system scores based on available data.
